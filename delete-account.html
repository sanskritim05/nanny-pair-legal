Delete your NannyPair account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open NannyPair and log in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email support@nannypair.app from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my NannyPair account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your login, profile and photo, children and their care notes, location, messages you sent, connections, saved items, blocks, notifications and reviews you wrote. If you are in an active share, you leave it first and the other family and nanny are told.

What may be kept

Nothing is kept in the live service. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
