NannyPair Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Short version: we collect what's needed to match nearby families and nannies. We never show your exact location or address, we don't sell your data, and there are no ads. You can delete your account and data at any time in the app.

Who we are

NannyPair helps families who live close to each other share one nanny, and helps nannies find those families. Contact: support@nannypair.app.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Profile: your name, an optional photo, and what you write about yourself. Families add their care days and hours, budget, hosting preference and optional preferences. Nannies add their rate, experience, availability, age groups and certifications.
  • Children: each child's birth month and year only (no names or photos), plus optional care notes such as allergies and medications.
  • Location: the location you allow from your phone or pick on a map. It is used to find matches within about 1 mile.
  • Messages, plans and reviews: chats with connected families and your share, the share plan you agree on, offers, and reviews.
  • Safety: reports and blocks you make, and the outcome of nanny ID checks.

We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone or camera recordings, advertising IDs, or payment details.

How location is protected

Your precise location is stored in a private table that only your own account can read. Everyone else sees a rough area that is moved 200 to 400 meters from your real spot, and distances only as bands such as "under 0.5 mi". Your address is only shared if you choose to type it in a chat.

Who can see what

  • Nearby families see your first name, photo, kids' ages, schedule, budget, preferences and rough area.
  • Children's care notes are visible only to your own family, the other family in your share, and your share's nanny.
  • Your email is never shown to other users.
  • Reviews of nannies are public on their profile. Reviews between families are private and only seen by the NannyPair team.
  • The NannyPair team can see accounts, reports and reviews to keep the service safe.

How we use it

To run matching, chat, shares and reviews; to verify nannies; to handle reports and keep people safe; and to fix problems. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell or rent it.

Service providers

Our data is stored and processed by Supabase (database, login, file storage and realtime chat). Maps are shown with Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone, which receive the map area being viewed. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules limit each person to the data they are allowed to see.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at Profile > Delete my account, or by following these steps. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, children and care notes, location, messages you sent, connections, saved items, blocks, notifications and reviews you wrote. If you are in an active share, you leave it first. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Children

NannyPair is for adults (18+). Parents provide limited information about their children (birth month and year, and optional care notes) to arrange care. Children do not use the app.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, hide yourself from the map, turn off "open to new jobs", block people, and delete your account at any time. To ask about or get a copy of your data, email support@nannypair.app.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
